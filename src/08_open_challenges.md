## SECTION 8 — Known Gaps and Open Challenges

**8a — Open Challenges** `[OPEN]`

**8a.1** *The Undetected Comosí Ceiling*: JIT-OM and Polyphonic Encoding
address detected divergence and proactively reduce transmission risk.
Neither eliminates the possibility of undetected axiomatic divergence
persisting beneath a functioning comosí. No current mechanism within the
GMRTI can surface a divergence that both parties' covenants are
successfully routing around. This is a structural limitation, not a
solvable problem within the framework's current architecture.

**8a.2** *The Primitive Covenant Transmission Problem*: Establishing the
Primitive Covenant (2e.5ii) between agents who do not already share it is
itself a transmission problem. No Covenant Zero Protocol for bootstrapping
the Primitive Covenant from the Minimal Substrate currently exists.

**8a.3** *The Compiling Overhead Problem*: balancing the friction of
Rationally Agnostic reconstruction against the need for generative creative
flow. High-DRC disciplines can absorb this overhead; the framework's
applicability to lower-DRC creative and philosophical work without
prohibitive cost remains an ongoing optimization target.

**8a.4** *The Convergence Proof Gap*: Dynamic Re-Anchoring (2d.2) and the
Maintenance Covenant (2d.2) lack formal convergence guarantees. A proof that
anchor oscillation remains bounded under specified correction-stream conditions
is needed.

**8a.5** *The Cognitive Overhead Reflexivity Problem*: GMRTI criticizes
existing approaches for cognitive overhead (R2 Refinery) while its own
operational surface — 8 operators, 5-stage algorithm, JIT-OM, PSP, BTE,
DRC, Meta-DRC, polyphonic encoding, Core Path Minimization, Dynamic
Re-Anchoring, Temporal Stranger Protocol — is at least as complex.
Empirical validation that Self-Annotation is cognitively tractable is
needed before the framework's overhead critique can be sustained
without tu quoque.
