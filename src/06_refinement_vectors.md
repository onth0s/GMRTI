## SECTION 6 — The GMRTI in Operation: Refinement Vectors

**6a — The Self-Application Principle** `[STABLE][RECURSIVE]`

`[PLAIN]` A method for clear thinking must be able to test itself. Everything
the GMRTI recommends for other ideas is also used to test and refine this
document over time.

**6a.1** The GMRTI is not exempt from its own prescriptions. As a framework
    transmitted through language, it is subject to the same divergence risks it
    describes. This is not a weakness but a feature: the GMRTI's own development
    is the most natural test case for its methodology.

**6a.2** This means iterations of the GMRTI document — including this one — are
    Covenant Declarations subject to Generative Reconstruction and Divergence
    Localization by adversarial readers, including future versions of the
    authors themselves.

**6a.3** The framework's commitment to self-application is partially executed in
    the current iteration: adversarial audit (`refinery/DISSONANCE.md`)
    performed
    Mirror-mode reconstruction and Divergence Localization against declared
    claims (Stages 2–3), with resulting repairs carried out via the Ratified
    Execution Protocol (`DECISIONS.md`, `PLAN.md`) under direct authorial
    oversight rather than the algorithm's own Stage 4 operations. A complete
    self-audit cycle — running Stage 4 Targeted Repair and, where applicable,
    Stage 5 Irreducibility Declaration as the algorithm itself specifies them,
    on a single section — remains a validation target.
    `[PROVISIONAL][RECURSIVE]`

**6b — The LLM-Manifesto Iteration Loop** `[PROVISIONAL]`

**6b.1** One operationalization of the GMRTI's Refinement front is the following
    cycle:

1. **Declaration**: Produce a committed, explicit articulation of the framework.
2. **Adversarial Critique**: Subject the declaration to critique by a Mirror
   calibrated to the document's DRC — operationally, an LLM prompted to identify
   architectural failures, Anchor-Only Subgraphs, Edge Divergences, and internal
   contradictions, with interpretive charity minimized.
3. **Architectural Reconstruction**: Rewrite the declaration addressing major
   structural failures, not merely patching surface inconsistencies.
4. **Coherence Audit**: Request an exhaustive audit asking not merely "where
   does this fail" but "what is the full anatomy of its failure modes and what
   would maximum cohesion look like."
5. **Irreducibility Check**: After each full iteration, explicitly identify
   which axiomatic commitments have been preserved, which have been silently
   replaced, and whether replacements represent genuine improvements or
   undetected covenant intrusions by the LLM's own priors. This check must
   include: (a) a word-for-word comparison of all vocabulary definitions against
   the prior iteration, since definitional softening is the most invisible form
   of covenant intrusion; and (b) a length-and-redundancy audit of all working
   artifacts in the repository's `refinery/` workspace, since Refinery
   compression is a confirmed failure mode.
6. **Return to step 1** with the refined declaration.


**6b.2** The LLM functions as an adversarial receiving covenant with
    structurally reduced interpretive charity — surfacing Anchor-Only Subgraph
    and Edge Divergence failures that a charitable reader would paper over.
    `[PROVISIONAL]`

**6b.3** Known limitation: the LLM's critique will reliably surface failures of
    *transmission* but is less reliable for failures of *conception* —
    particularly where the LLM's priors share the author's axiomatic mistake.
    The Irreducibility Check in step 5 is the primary mitigation. `[STABLE]`

**6b.4 — Compression as Covenant Intrusion and the Burden-of-Proof Rule**
`[STABLE]`

A specific failure mode of the LLM-Manifesto loop, observed empirically across
this document's iteration history: LLM adversarial readers with trained priors
favoring brevity will systematically read Polyphonic Encoding as
redundancy-in-the-pejorative-sense and compress it out. This is not editorial
improvement — it is the LLM's aesthetic axiomatic cluster silently replacing the
author's epistemological one.

**The Compression Asymmetry Justification**: The epistemic asymmetry favoring
preservation over compression is directly derived from the foundational purpose
of Polyphonic Encoding (§2a, §2f). Ex ante, a transmitting covenant cannot know
which specific topological route will successfully achieve comosí with an
unknown receiver's existing edge-structure. The cost of retaining an extra
independent path is minimal (marginal document length), whereas the cost of
erroneously cutting a path is catastrophic (total transmission failure for all
receivers whose topology required that specific route). Therefore, under
epistemic uncertainty, the default presumption strictly favors path
preservation.

**The Compression Burden-of-Proof Rule**: To distinguish legitimate editorial
tightening from destructive compression, GMRTI establishes a strict
burden-of-proof standard. A proposed cut or compression is legitimate *if and
only if* the removed path is demonstrated under Core Path Minimization (§2f.6)
to be both *destination-isomorphic* and *non-route-independent* with a path that
survives the edit — proving it to be true duplicate redundancy rather than an
alternative entry ramp. Any editorial pruning that cannot make this explicit
showing is classified as presumptive covenant intrusion and rejected.

- **6b.4i — The Art Tutorials Allegory** `[ALLEGORY: COMPRESSION-ASYMMETRY]`
  Consider online art tutorials for drawing hands or perspective. Nobody looks
  at the thousands of drawing tutorials and says "we already have one good
  explanation, delete the rest." One instructor explains perspective using
  railroad tracks, another using cardboard boxes, and another using horizon
  lines and camera lenses. Because every beginner starts with a different
  background, no single tutorial works for everyone. Trimming away alternative
  explanations to save space just ensures that learners whose minds worked like
  the deleted tutorial will fail to learn.

The symptom is: successive iterations become shorter while appearing more
coherent. The diagnosis: Redundancy-Masking at the meta-level — the document's
transmission fidelity appears to improve (tighter, cleaner) while its actual
structural content degrades (fewer independent routes, invisible voids). The
mitigation: the Irreducibility Check must explicitly include a
length-and-redundancy audit, verifying that compression has not stripped
Polyphonic Encoding rather than eliminated genuine repetition.

This failure mode was confirmed empirically in v0.6: the Refinery entries R2
through R4 lost their elaborated open questions, and the metaagnosticism
definition lost its second clause. Both losses made the document read more
cleanly while reducing its precision and depth. The v0.7 repairs restore both.
Future iterations should treat any reduction in Refinery entry length as a flag
requiring explicit justification. `[STABLE]`

**6c — The Temporal Stranger Protocol** `[STABLE]`

**6c.1** The Self-to-Future-Self transmission mode (1c.1) has a specific
    operationalization: the *Temporal Stranger Protocol*. The practitioner
    treats their past notes, documents, or articulations as the output of a
    Foreign Mind — a Temporal Stranger — and applies the full GMRTI algorithm to
    the task of reconstructing what that mind intended.

**6c.2** This protocol reverses the default reading mode, in which a reader
    assumes continuity of covenant with their past self and assimilates past
    work through the current covenant without checking for drift. The Temporal
    Stranger Protocol instead:

- **6c.2i** Treats past documents as Covenant Declarations from a *t₁* covenant
    that may differ significantly from the current *t₂* covenant.
- **6c.2ii** Performs Generative Reconstruction on those documents, attempting
    to recover the *t₁* edge-structure rather than projecting the *t₂*
    interpretation onto it.
- **6c.2iii** Produces a divergence report identifying where *t₁* and *t₂*
    edge-structures conflict — which is a direct map of the covenant's drift
    over the intervening period.

**6c.3** The practical value is twofold: it prevents the illusion of
    intellectual continuity where genuine cognitive drift has occurred, and it
    surfaces which of the past self's axiomatic commitments have been silently
    abandoned — which is often where the most significant intellectual
    development has happened, and therefore where the most important insights
    are waiting to be explicitly recognized. `[STABLE]`
