# THE CONSTRUCTO-PRAGMATIST DEFINITION

# Hopefully Simple Description

The Constructo-Pragmatist Definition (CPD) is a method for turning an abstract concept into a precise, executable specification.

Instead of asking only “What does this concept really mean?”, CPD asks:

“What would we have to specify so that different people can reconstruct this concept sufficiently similarly, know how it operates, and act accordingly?”

It does this by defining the concept's preconditions, states, transitions, invariants, enforcement mechanism, consequences, and termination conditions.

The goal is not to prove that a definition corresponds to some Platonic essence. The goal is to maximize semantic covenant convergence: make the concept sufficiently clear that different semantic covenants can understand and coordinate around the same operational object.

For example, a valid chess resignation is not defined merely as “a player says they resign.” A proper CPD specifies who can resign, when they can do so, what state the game enters, how the resignation is recognized, what result it produces, and when the resulting protocol terminates.

In short: CPD treats definitions as things that must be capable of being understood, instantiated, executed, and terminated—not merely stated.

# Thesis Abstract 

The Constructo-Pragmatist Definition: Architectural Specification of an Operational Abstraction

Abstract concepts frequently govern concrete reasoning and action despite being communicated through definitions whose operational consequences remain implicit. The Constructo-Pragmatist Definition (CPD) proposes that, for abstractions intended to function within a domain, definition should be treated as an architectural specification rather than solely as the identification of a semantic essence.

CPD combines a constructivist requirement—that an abstraction be reconstructible through explicitly specified relational structure—with a pragmatist requirement—that the adequacy of this specification be assessed by its capacity to produce semantic covenant convergence within a declared operational domain. A CPD therefore specifies irreducible preconditions, an operational state space, state transitions, invariants, enforcement architecture, yield, and terminal conditions. Where necessary, it additionally specifies self-consumption and institutional dissolution mechanisms to prevent an operational apparatus from perpetuating itself beyond the conditions that justified it.

The framework does not equate operational consequences with metaphysical truth. Rather, it treats operational precision as valuable because it enables distinct semantic covenants to reconstruct sufficiently the same abstraction and consequently achieve Comosí: functional convergence sufficient for coordinated inference and action without requiring total semantic or ontological agreement.

The framework is situated downstream of the General Method for Refinement and Transmission of Ideas (GMRTI). GMRTI addresses refinement, reconstruction, and transmission of semantic objects; CPD addresses their subsequent transformation into bounded operational architectures.

A worked example, valid chess resignation, demonstrates the distinction. The abstraction is not reduced to the utterance “I resign”; its CPD specifies the relevant participants, game states, transitions, invariants, governing enforcement mechanism, resulting game state, and terminal condition. The example illustrates the central thesis: a useful operational definition is one that gives distinct semantic covenants enough relational and procedural structure to understand what the same abstraction permits, requires, produces, and terminates.

### Architectural Specification of an Operational Abstraction

## Core Axiom

An abstraction is not a Platonic truth waiting to be discovered; it is a **generative, bounded protocol for constructing, transmitting, and coordinating around a semantic object**.

A Constructo-Pragmatist Definition (CPD) specifies the exact structural, procedural, and operational conditions required for an abstraction to be instantiated within a declared domain.

Its definition is therefore inseparable from its **material footprint, execution constraints, enforcement conditions, and terminal yield**.

The purpose of CPD is not to establish metaphysical essence. It is to **maximize semantic covenant convergence**: to make it possible for distinct semantic covenants to reconstruct sufficiently the same abstraction such that their resulting inferences and actions converge within a declared operational domain.

Accordingly:

> **A CPD is adequate insofar as its executable architecture enables sufficiently convergent reconstruction, inference, and action among participating semantic covenants.**

This is a pragmatic criterion of usefulness for understanding, not a claim that usefulness constitutes metaphysical truth.

# §1 — CONSTRUCTIVIST REQUIREMENT

### §1.1 — Edge-Primacy

The semantic content of an abstraction resides in its **relational structure**, not in isolated atomic nodes.

Relations may be:

* causal,
* inferential,
* procedural,
* normative,
* temporal,
* constitutive,
* or otherwise operationally relevant.

An abstraction is consequently represented as an emergent density within a relational topology.

The CPD must therefore specify the relations necessary for reconstructing the abstraction rather than merely supplying a nominal label for it.

### §1.2 — Syntactic Execution

A CPD functions as a **syntactic constraint on reconstruction**.

Its specification must not depend upon unstated contextual knowledge that materially determines its operation.

The receiving covenant must be forced, by the structure of the specification, through the relevant relational paths.

This extends GMRTI's principle of **Syntactic Edge-Forcing** into operational definition: the definition does not merely describe the relations constituting the abstraction; its form constrains the receiver toward their reconstruction.

### §1.3 — Structural Non-Exemption

The constraints constituting the abstraction must be mechanically embedded in its architecture rather than dependent upon the continued goodwill, interpretation, or exceptional status of the initiating agent.

Where a constraint applies to an agent, the architecture must specify how that constraint applies **to the initiator as well as to other participating agents**.

A rule which exists only because its author promises to obey it is not structurally instantiated.

---

# §2 — PRAGMATIST REQUIREMENT

## §2.1 — Operational Semantics

The operational meaning of a constructo-pragmatist abstraction is determined by the **operations it licenses and the material consequences produced when those operations are executed within the declared domain**.

Declared intention cannot substitute for runtime behavior.

This does **not** assert:

> “Whatever happens is therefore the true meaning.”

Rather:

> “If an abstraction is intended to function as an operational object, its specification must account for what execution of that object actually permits, prohibits, changes, and terminates.”

The distinction is important: CPD operationalizes meaning without collapsing semantic identity into observed behavior.

---

## §2.2 — Semantic Covenant Convergence

The primary pragmatic purpose of CPD is to **maximize semantic covenant convergence**.

Given participating semantic covenants \(C_1,\ldots,C_n\), a CPD seeks to minimize materially relevant divergence in their reconstruction of the defined abstraction:

$$
CPD(A,D) \rightarrow
\operatorname{Convergence}(C_1,\ldots,C_n;A,D)
$$

where:

* \(A\) = abstraction,
* \(D\) = declared operational domain,
* \(C_i\) = participating semantic covenant.

The desired result is not necessarily identical total semantic content.

Rather, the participating covenants should converge sufficiently that, under the relevant boundary conditions:

$$
\operatorname{Action}_{C_1}(A)
\cong
\operatorname{Action}_{C_2}(A)
\cong
\cdots
\cong
\operatorname{Action}_{C_n}(A)
$$

where \(\cong\) denotes **operational congruence**, not ontological or semantic identity.

Thus CPD inherits GMRTI's **Comosí** principle:

> Sufficient agreement for coordinated operation does not require total agreement about everything the abstraction means.

The point of defining something precisely is therefore not merely to make a proposition *true-looking*. It is to make the abstraction **usefully intelligible across covenants**.

---

## §2.3 — Transactional Cost

Definition and observation are physical and epistemic transactions.

Asserting a semantic boundary changes the coordinate system through which participating agents classify and act upon the relevant domain.

A CPD must therefore account, where materially relevant, for:

* the cost of acquiring the information required to execute it;
* the effects of its execution upon participating agents;
* institutional and incentive effects;
* feedback produced by execution;
* and changes to the relational topology produced by the abstraction itself.

A definition is not an inert description when its execution changes the system it describes.

---

## §2.4 — Operational Parking

CPD terminates at **bounded operational convergence**, not universal philosophical consensus.

Where remaining divergence does not alter the actions, inferences, or outcomes relevant to the declared domain, that divergence may be operationally parked.

This is not an assertion that the divergence is meaningless.

It is an explicit recognition that semantic reconstruction may terminate at:

> **“Different enough in metaphysics; identical enough in operation.”**

---

# §3 — ANATOMY OF A CONSTRUCTO-PRAGMATIST DEFINITION

A complete CPD consists of the following architectural components.

### I. Irreducible Preconditions \(P\)

The minimum semantic, environmental, institutional, or material conditions required before the abstraction can be instantiated.

These define the substrate upon which execution occurs.

### II. State Space \(\Sigma\)

The set of operationally relevant states through which the abstraction can pass.

States must be sufficiently differentiated to prevent materially relevant ambiguity.

### III. Transition Function \(T\)

The permitted state transitions:

$$
T:\Sigma \times I \rightarrow \Sigma
$$

where \(I\) represents relevant inputs.

The transition structure must specify chronology where chronology is constitutive.

### IV. Invariants \(I\)

Conditions which must remain true throughout execution.

An invariant cannot depend merely upon an agent remembering to preserve it.

### V. Enforcement Architecture \(E\)

The mechanism through which the abstraction acquires operational force **given the governing architecture of its declared domain**.

\(E\) need not be metaphysically ultimate.

It specifies the actual mechanism by which violations, transitions, or terminal conditions acquire consequences independently of the initiating agent's momentary preference.

### VI. Yield \(Y\)

The operational consequences produced by successful execution.

Yield describes what the abstraction **does**, not what an author claims it ought to do.

### VII. Terminal Condition \(\Omega\)

The condition under which the instantiated abstraction terminates.

Termination applies to the **instantiated abstraction**, not necessarily to every institutional or causal structure surrounding it.

This distinction prevents a bounded protocol from being required to dissolve the entire environment in which it operates.

---

# §4 — SELF-CONSUMING CLAUSE

Where the abstraction involves the exercise of concentrated power, the CPD may require a **Self-Consuming Clause**.

Its purpose is to prevent the mechanism used to instantiate the abstraction from becoming a permanent independent object.

Formally:

$$
Power_{instantiate}(A)
\rightarrow
Cost_{initiator}
$$

rather than:

$$
Power_{instantiate}(A)
\rightarrow
Power_{permanent}
$$

The mechanism must specify how its own authority is exhausted, transferred, or terminated.

### Architectural boundary

CPD does **not** require infinite recursive specification of every mechanism that enforces its own termination.

A declared architectural boundary may treat lower-level enforcement or termination mechanisms as primitives.

Otherwise:

$$
E_1 \rightarrow E_2 \rightarrow E_3 \rightarrow \cdots
$$

produces an infinite regress rather than an executable definition.

The CPD must therefore state where recursive specification terminates.

---

# §5 — INSTITUTIONAL DISSOLUTION

Where an abstraction creates an apparatus whose continued existence could reproduce the conditions that originally justified it, the CPD must specify its dissolution.

This is distinct from the terminal condition of an ordinary bounded instance.

The requirement is:

$$
ContextExpired(A)
\Rightarrow
Dissolve(Apparatus_A)
$$

rather than:

$$
ContextExpired(A)
\Rightarrow
Apparatus_A \text{ remains available indefinitely}
$$

Institutional dissolution therefore functions as an anti-perpetuation constraint.

---

# §6 — DOMAIN RELATIVITY AND MATERIALITY

“Materially relevant” is necessarily **domain-relative**.

A divergence is material when changing that divergence can alter the permitted transitions, invariants, enforcement, yield, or terminal condition of the abstraction within the declared domain.

Thus:

$$
Material_D(\Delta)
$$

means that semantic divergence \(\Delta\) is material **with respect to domain \(D\)**.

CPD does not require every possible semantic distinction to be resolved.

It requires the distinctions that can change the abstraction's operational behavior to be resolved sufficiently for convergence.

---

# §7 — DISPUTE PROTOCOL

A CPD does not make ordinary semantic disagreement invalid.

Disagreement may identify:

1. transmission failure;
2. reconstruction failure;
3. internal contradiction;
4. omitted operational conditions;
5. materially divergent interpretations;
6. irreducible axiomatic divergence.

However, a **replacement claim** is stronger than an objection.

To claim that a CPD should be replaced by an alternative operational definition, the challenger must provide:

1. an alternative state-transition architecture;
2. a mechanically specified non-exemption mechanism where required;
3. a mechanism preventing semantic drift or unintended perpetuation where those are relevant to the abstraction;
4. sufficient specification to determine whether the alternative actually produces greater operational convergence.

Failure to provide a runnable alternative does not prove the existing definition true.

It means only that the proposed replacement has not yet supplied an executable competing architecture.

---

# §8 — THE GMRTI → CPD PIPELINE

CPD is downstream of semantic refinement rather than a substitute for it.

The general architecture is:

$$
\boxed{
Covenant
\rightarrow
Transmission
\rightarrow
Reconstruction
\rightarrow
Specification
\rightarrow
Instantiation
\rightarrow
Runtime
\rightarrow
Yield
\rightarrow
Termination
}
$$

GMRTI primarily addresses the conditions under which the semantic object can be **refined and transmitted**.

CPD addresses what happens when that reconstructed abstraction is required to become an **operational object**.

Thus CPD can be viewed as the architectural specialization of GMRTI for concepts whose meaning is materially instantiated through action.

# §9 — WORKED INSTANTIATION: VALID CHESS RESIGNATION

Consider the abstraction:

> **“A valid chess resignation.”**

The target abstraction remains abstract.

**Chess is the operational domain in which the abstraction is instantiated.**

The point is not to discover the metaphysical essence of “resignation.” The point is to construct a specification sufficiently precise that agents who intend to play proper chess can converge on what counts as one.

---

## §9.1 — Preconditions \(P\)

The minimum substrate includes:

* a legally constituted chess game;
* identifiable players;
* an active game state;
* a governing ruleset;
* a mechanism capable of recognizing and recording resignation.

Without an active game, “resignation” cannot instantiate the same abstraction.

---

## §9.2 — State Space \(\Sigma\)

For example:

$$
\Sigma =
\{
GameActive,
ResignationDeclared,
ResignationRecorded,
GameTerminated
\}
$$

The states distinguish the act from its recognition and terminal consequence.

---

## §9.3 — Transition Function \(T\)

A simplified execution path:

$$
GameActive
\rightarrow
ResignationDeclared
\rightarrow
ResignationRecorded
\rightarrow
GameTerminated
$$

No transition permits:

$$
GameTerminated
\rightarrow
GameActive
$$

within the same resignation instance.

The chronology is constitutive because a resignation that occurs after termination is not the same operational event.

---

## §9.4 — Invariants \(I\)

Relevant invariants include:

* only a participant in the game can resign;
* a spectator cannot resign on behalf of a player merely by declaring it;
* the resignation must be attributable to the relevant player;
* once validly recorded, the resignation produces the prescribed game result;
* the resignation cannot be converted into an ordinary continuation of the same game.

These aren't merely descriptions. They constrain execution.

---

## §9.5 — Enforcement \(E\)

The governing chess architecture supplies the enforcement mechanism through which the abstraction acquires operational force.

The relevant rules and arbiter/tournament procedures determine whether an attempted resignation is recognized and what consequence follows.

The CPD therefore does **not** claim that the rules of chess are metaphysically true.

It specifies:

> Given the governing architecture of chess, this is the mechanism through which “valid resignation” becomes operationally binding.

That distinction is precisely the pragmatist move.

---

## §9.6 — Yield \(Y\)

The yield is the game-level consequence prescribed for a valid resignation:

$$
ValidResignation
\Rightarrow
GameTermination
$$

with the corresponding result recorded.

The current board position does not determine whether the resignation itself is valid merely because the position is strategically winning or losing.

That information may be relevant to the surrounding game, but it is not constitutive of the resignation protocol.

---

## §9.7 — Terminal Condition \(\Omega\)

The instantiated abstraction terminates when the resignation has produced the prescribed game termination and result.

$$
\Omega =
GameTerminated
$$

This does **not** require the dissolution of chess as an institution.

The game terminates.

Chess, its rules, arbiters, tournaments, and future games do not.

That distinction is precisely why \(\Omega\) must be understood as the terminal condition of the **instantiation**, rather than an instruction to dissolve its entire surrounding environment.

---

# §10 — WHY THE CHESS EXAMPLE MATTERS

The example demonstrates what CPD is actually trying to accomplish.

Suppose two players both say:

> “I know what resignation means.”

That statement tells us relatively little.

One might mean:

> “I verbally announce that I give up.”

Another might mean:

> “The game is thereby terminated and the prescribed result is recorded.”

A third might think:

> “I can announce resignation after the game has already ended.”

The CPD forces the relevant distinctions into the architecture.

The participants do not need identical philosophies of surrender, agency, games, language, or even victory.

They need sufficient convergence on:

$$
P,\Sigma,T,I,E,Y,\Omega
$$

such that **when the relevant event occurs, they know what happens next.**

That is the Constructo-Pragmatist criterion in miniature:

> **A good definition does not merely tell you what a word supposedly points at. It gives different semantic covenants enough structure to reconstruct the same operational object and consequently coordinate around it.**

And that is why the *Pragmatist* half is doing real work here: **the value of the definition lies in what its precision makes possible for understanding and coordinated action—not in its possession of some independently certified Platonic essence.**
