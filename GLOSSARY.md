# Glossary

The following terms carry precise technical meaning throughout this document and
must not be substituted with natural-language synonyms without explicit
redefinition:

#### * GMRTI
General Method for Refinement and Transmission of Ideas `[STABLE]`

#### * GMCR
General Method for Conflict Resolution (downstream application of [[GMRTI]])
`[STABLE]`

#### * Constructo-Pragmatist Definition (CPD)
The downstream architectural specification framework that converts a refined
semantic abstraction into a bounded, executable operational protocol within a
declared domain, evaluated by its capacity to produce operational congruence
among participating [[Semantic Covenant|covenants]] using the [[CPD 7-Tuple]].
`[PROVISIONAL]`

#### * CPD 7-Tuple
The formal anatomy of a [[Constructo-Pragmatist Definition (CPD)]]:
preconditions $P$, state space $\Sigma$, transition function $T$, invariants
$I$, enforcement architecture $E$, yield $Y$, and terminal condition $\Omega$.
`[PROVISIONAL]`

#### * Self-Consuming Clause
An architectural constraint within a [[Constructo-Pragmatist Definition (CPD)]]
specifying that the concentrated power or authority required to instantiate an
abstraction is exhausted or transferred upon execution, preventing the
initiating apparatus from becoming a permanent independent object.
`[PROVISIONAL]`

#### * Institutional Dissolution
The anti-perpetuation constraint within a [[Constructo-Pragmatist Definition
(CPD)]] mandating that when the context justifying an operational apparatus
expires, the apparatus itself is dissolved rather than persisting indefinitely.
`[PROVISIONAL]`

#### * Semantic Covenant
(*convenio semántico*) — The totality of meaning-relations held by a rational
entity at a given moment; a personal, circumstantial, and mutable directed
graph. Not a fixed dictionary but a living topology. `[STABLE]`

#### * Comosí
The functional overlap between two [[Semantic Covenant|semantic covenants]]
sufficient to produce apparent understanding within a bounded domain, without
guaranteeing identity of underlying edge-structure. `[STABLE]`

#### * Apodictic Comosí
A functional overlap between [[Semantic Covenant|covenants]] so tightly
constrained by [[Syntactic Edge-Forcing]] (e.g., formal mathematical notation)
that residual [[Semantic Entropy]] is negligible relative to natural language
within a trained community. `[STABLE]`

#### * Metaagnosticism
The epistemological position that: (a) precise definitions are structurally
unavailable due to the circular, self-referential nature of
language-as-symbol-system; and (b) the impossibility of verifying whether
certainty has been achieved is itself a first-order epistemic condition, not
merely a practical limitation. The *meta* in metaagnosticism names precisely
this second clause: not only is certainty unavailable, but the verification of
whether one has achieved it is also unavailable — making the epistemic limit
operate at one level above ordinary fallibilism. `[STABLE][RECURSIVE][RESTORED]`

#### * Semantic Entropy
The log-measure of structural ambiguity in a transmitted signal, denoted as
$H_s(S)$. Represents the log-count of distinct functional equivalence classes of
receiving-covenant edge-configurations compatible with the signal under the
[[Graph-Space Partitioning Axiom]] (a class $[G_R]$ is compatible with $S$ iff
at least one representative $G \in [G_R]$ satisfies $S$). In practice, it is
operationalized via the [[Behavioral Convergence Proxy]] (Path-Count Metric).
`[STABLE]` [provisional dependency boundary: §3c.3, §8b.1]

#### * Behavioral Convergence Proxy
The operational path-count metric for [[Semantic Entropy]]: the number of
independent [[Polyphonic Encoding]] paths that must be successfully
reconstructed and verified by the receiver under Audit-Mode [[Domain Risk
Coefficient (DRC)|DRC]] before reaching the [[Operational Action Threshold]].
`[STABLE]` [provisional dependency boundary: §3c.3, §8b.1]

#### * Graph-Space Partitioning Axiom
The formal individuation criterion resolving §8b.1: under Audit-Mode exact
equivalence ($\varepsilon = 0$ on a declared finite operational probe suite),
functional equivalence is transitive and discretizes candidate
receiving-covenant graph space into a genuine quotient partition of equivalence
classes $\{[G_R]\}$ (two topologies belong to the same class if and only if they
license indistinguishable downstream operational inferences across all declared
probes). Under tolerance-bounded [[Comosí|comosí]] ($\varepsilon > 0$),
functional congruence is reflexive and symmetric but non-transitive, inducing a
tolerance covering rather than disjoint equivalence classes. `[PROVISIONAL]`
[provisional dependency boundary: §3c.3]

#### * Transmission
The attempted transfer of semantic content from one [[Semantic
Covenant|covenant]] to another, or from one temporal state of a covenant to a
later state of the same covenant. `[STABLE]`

#### * Refinement
The iterative process of improving the internal coherence and transmissibility
of a framework within and across [[Semantic Covenant|semantic covenants]].
`[STABLE]`

#### * Divergence
Any detectable mismatch between two [[Semantic Covenant|covenants']] handling of
the same conceptual region. `[STABLE]`

#### * Surface Subgraph
An edge-cluster in a [[Semantic Covenant]] that is highly context-dependent,
easily renegotiated, and minimally load-bearing; repairable through single-path
clarification (§2b). `[STABLE]`

#### * Structural Subgraph
An edge-cluster that routes meaning between large regions of a [[Semantic
Covenant]]; revision produces downstream effects on dependent [[Surface
Subgraph|Surface Subgraphs]] (§2b). `[STABLE]`

#### * Axiomatic Edge-Cluster
An edge-cluster with maximal structural centrality within a [[Semantic
Covenant]], flagged as *Axiomatically Active* by the [[Dependency-Tracing
Scan]]; alteration requires rebuilding the covenant's identity (§2b). `[STABLE]`

#### * Anchor-Only Subgraph
A divergence type (formerly Severe Edge Deficit) in which the receiver holds a
shared surface label with zero internal relational depth attached to it,
requiring [[Polyphonic Encoding]] to construct internal topology (§2c.1i).
`[STABLE]`

#### * Temporal Drift Divergence
Divergence arising not from difference between two people, but from a single
[[Semantic Covenant]] changing over time between encoding ($t_1$) and decoding
($t_2$), addressed by the [[Temporal Stranger]] protocol (§2c.1iii). `[STABLE]`

#### * Federated Graph
The distributed, externalized semantic structure of an institution or community,
stabilized via [[Artifact Anchor|Artifact Anchors]] rather than collective
consciousness. `[PROVISIONAL]`

#### * Artifact Anchor
An external, relatively immutable scaffold (written law, canonical text,
standardized protocol) that forces constituent individual [[Semantic
Covenant|covenants]] to continuously realign their [[Structural
Subgraph|Structural Subgraphs]] to a shared artificial topology. `[PROVISIONAL]`

#### * Domain Risk Coefficient (DRC)
A context-sensitive parameter defined on the bounded interval $[0.0, 1.0]$
governing the required precision of reconstruction; inversely proportional to
interpretive charity. Calibrating DRC toward $0.0$ maximizes interpretive
charity (Generative Mode); calibrating DRC toward $1.0$ drives charity toward
zero and enforces strict verification (Audit Mode via [[The Mirror]]). The
assignment of numerical DRC values to specific domains is deliberately
un-prescribed by [[GMRTI]], as risk ranking requires normative value judgments
that belong exclusively to the participating covenants. `[STABLE]`

#### * Meta-DRC Divergence
A disagreement between communicating covenants regarding the [[Domain Risk
Coefficient (DRC)]] classification of a domain, which pauses primary
[[Transmission|transmission]] and defaults to the highest demanded DRC of any
participating party (given a declared commensurate stake) until resolved.
`[STABLE]`

#### * Rational Agnosticism
The operational posture of deliberate suspension in which a receiver treats
themselves as possessing only the edges explicitly declared in the current
transmission, surfacing hidden contextual assumptions (§2e). `[STABLE]`

#### * Just-In-Time Ontological Marking (JIT-OM)
The practice of deploying ontological type distinctions retroactively and
locally, only at the precise site of a detected [[Divergence|Edge Divergence]],
rather than as upfront metalanguage. `[STABLE]`

#### * Polyphonic Encoding
The deliberate provision of multiple structurally independent edge-paths to the
same semantic destination within a single [[Transmission|transmission]] act. The
primary proactive transmission fidelity strategy. `[STABLE]`

#### * Allegory (Worked Instantiation)
A concrete, domain-bounded [[Polyphonic Encoding|polyphonic-encoding]] path
whose intermediate nodes and edges reside in an accessible operational domain,
preserving destination-isomorphism with an abstract target concept
($\operatorname{Action}_C(A) \cong
\operatorname{Action}_C(\operatorname{Allegory}(A))$) under [[Core Path
Minimization]] (§2f.6) to reduce [[Semantic Entropy]]. `[STABLE]` [provisional
dependency boundary: §7d.1]

#### * Redundancy-Masking
The failure mode in which successful [[Polyphonic Encoding]] conceals a local
[[Anchor-Only Subgraph]] that would produce transmission failure under
single-path conditions. `[STABLE]`

#### * Path-Stripping Probing
The diagnostic protocol for detecting [[Redundancy-Masking]] by temporarily
disabling all but one encoding path to check if the receiver's reconstruction
still satisfies the [[Operational Action Threshold]]. `[STABLE]` [provisional
dependency boundary: §3c.3]

#### * Proactive Chaotic Probing (PCP)
The scheduled maintenance protocol that runs [[Path-Stripping Probing]] on
random samples of polyphonically encoded nodes and candidate disjunctive hubs
during audit states to surface latent divergence before real-time faults occur
(§2f.5v). `[STABLE]`

#### * Syntactic Edge-Forcing
A transmission fidelity mechanism in which the grammatical or compositional
structure of an expression compels the receiver to traverse a specific
relational path through semantic space in order to decode the expression. In the
v0.9 prototype, this is implemented by mandating canonical relational operators
(e.g. `[->causal]`, `[->inferential]`) in key structural claims. `[STABLE]`

#### * Edge Taxonomy
The partitioned classification of relation-types. Operates as a two-tiered
system: Tier 1 (Causal/Inferential/Other) for initial [[Covenant Declaration]],
and Tier 2 (8-type taxonomy) deployed locally via [[Just-In-Time Ontological
Marking (JIT-OM)|JIT-OM]] for [[Edge-Type Misattribution]] repair. `[STABLE]`

#### * Edge-Type Misattribution
A subtype of [[Divergence|Edge Divergence]] in which both covenants possess the
A-to-B relation but assign it to different edge-types in the [[Edge Taxonomy]] —
one treating it as inferential, the other as causal; one as normative, the other
as affective. Distinct from topological divergence within a shared edge-type,
and requiring a different repair strategy. `[STABLE]`

#### * Bad Actor
An adversarial participant who exploits interpretive charity by declaring
internally contradictory edge subgraphs, manufacturing spurious irreducibility,
demanding unbacked `DRC = 1.0`, or rejecting repairs verified by the
[[Topological Consistency Audit]] without supplying a distinguishing boundary
condition (§3g.4). `[STABLE]`

#### * Topological Consistency Audit
A diagnostic protocol to detect [[Bad Actor|Bad Actors]] by auditing the logical
consistency of their declared edge subgraphs and flagging contradictions or
bad-faith rejection of isomorphic repairs. `[STABLE]`

#### * Periodic Re-Mirroring Protocol
A methodological check to combat [[Artifact Anchor]] decay by periodically
evaluating the [[Federated Graph]] against the Anchor under [[The Mirror]]
posture to produce a desynchronization Delta Graph. `[PROVISIONAL]`

#### * Dependency-Tracing Scan
A scan of the topological network that calculates the downstream propagation
impact of altering specific edge-clusters, flagging those above a configurable
node threshold (default 30%, subject to empirical calibration) as [[Axiomatic
Edge-Cluster|Axiomatically Active]]. `[STABLE]`

#### * Boundary-Tracing Encapsulation
A formal containment wrapper drawn around a repaired subgraph that treats it as
a black-box node with declared interfaces, attenuating (not eliminating)
localized meaning-shifts from propagating holistically across the network.
Provides practical isolation bounded by interface edge count; residual holistic
leakage through interface-edge context change is a known structural limitation
(see 2h.3). `[STABLE]`

#### * Covenant Zero Protocol (CZP)
The ostensive association procedure that bootstraps the [[Primitive Covenant]]
(`causal`, `inferential`, `identity`) from the framework-independent [[Minimal
Substrate]] (§2e.5iii). `[STABLE]`

#### * Primitive Covenant
The GMRTI-specific shared substrate consisting of the three core primitive
relational operators (`[->causal]`, `[->inferential]`, `[->identity]`)
bootstrapped via [[Covenant Zero Protocol (CZP)|CZP]], with `[->procedural]`,
`[->referential]`, and `[->structural]` derived compositionally and
`[->normative]` and `[->affective]` negotiated via [[Just-In-Time Ontological
Marking (JIT-OM)|JIT-OM]] (see §2e.5ii), together with the edge-primacy
structural commitment and the Tier 1 coarse taxonomy. Required for
GMRTI-mediated communication specifically; sits above the framework-independent
[[Minimal Substrate]] (2e.5i). Its establishment between agents is itself a
transmission problem. `[STABLE]`

#### * Minimal Substrate
The weakest framework-independent assumption for structured communication:
agents can distinguish signals from non-signals, detect structural similarity,
and individuate their own conceptual regions. Carries no GMRTI-specific content.
`[STABLE]`

#### * Minimal Intelligibility Bootstrap
The stratified decoding substrate: the [[Minimal Substrate]]
(framework-independent) guarantees basic signal decoding; the [[Primitive
Covenant]] (GMRTI-specific) enables GMRTI-mediated repair. Calibrating [[Domain
Risk Coefficient (DRC)|DRC]] to maximum (1.0) reduces charity to the Minimal
Substrate baseline. `[STABLE]`

#### * Operational Action Threshold
The domain-specific threshold of reconstruction fidelity at which a receiver's
reconstructed semantic clustering produces downstream operational choices,
inferences, or behaviors that are functionally congruent with the transmission
across all declared boundary conditions. Serves as the termination criterion for
[[Operational Parking]] (§3a.1i) and [[Path-Stripping Probing]] (§2f.5iv).
`[PROVISIONAL]`

#### * Core Path Minimization
The complexity governor requiring that redundant paths in [[Polyphonic
Encoding]] maintain destination-isomorphism (terminating at the identical
semantic destination and licensing the same downstream inferences) while
preserving route-independence across disjoint intermediate topological
structures. `[STABLE]`

#### * Dynamic Re-Anchoring
The process of treating [[Artifact Anchor|Artifact Anchors]] as an exponentially
smoothed moving average, updated via continuous correction vectors to bound
tracking lag against textual drift (§4b.3). `[PROVISIONAL]`

#### * The Mirror
Any [[Rational Agnosticism|Rationally Agnostic]] receiver (human, LLM, or
future-self) operationalized to reconstruct a transmission using strictly
declared edges, with interpretive charity calibrated to the [[Domain Risk
Coefficient (DRC)]]. A tool within the [[GMRTI]] protocol, not the protocol
itself. `[STABLE]`

#### * Temporal Stranger
The past self treated as a Foreign Mind: the primary operationalization of
Self-to-Future-Self [[Transmission|transmission]] repair. `[STABLE]`

#### * Covenant Declaration
Stage 1 of the GMRTI algorithm: exposing the transmitting covenant's key
topological regions, edges, and Tier 1 coarse cuts so divergence can be detected
(§3b). `[STABLE]`

#### * Generative Reconstruction
Stage 2 of the GMRTI algorithm: Mirror mapping followed by DRC-calibrated
interpretive bridging, producing a candidate reconstruction and divergence
report (§3c). `[STABLE]`

#### * Divergence Localization
Stage 3 of the GMRTI algorithm: tracing detected divergence from [[Surface
Subgraph|Surface]] to [[Structural Subgraph|Structural]] to [[Axiomatic
Edge-Cluster|Axiomatic]] depth layers (§3d). `[STABLE]`

#### * Targeted Repair
Stage 4 of the GMRTI algorithm: applying Scaffolding, Local Alignment with
[[Boundary-Tracing Encapsulation]], [[Just-In-Time Ontological Marking
(JIT-OM)|JIT-OM]] type declaration, or Archival Reconstruction (§3e). `[STABLE]`

#### * Operational Parking
Algorithmic termination when reconstructed [[Comosí|comosí]] meets the
[[Operational Action Threshold]] for the bounded domain (§3a.1i). `[STABLE]`

#### * Cost-Bounded Termination
Algorithmic termination when the expected epistemic gain of further refinement
falls below the [[Domain Risk Coefficient (DRC)|DRC]]-adjusted cost (§3a.1iii,
§3g). `[STABLE]`

#### * Irreducibility Declaration
Stage 5 of the GMRTI algorithm: provisional formal acknowledgment that a
divergence at the [[Axiomatic Edge-Cluster|Axiomatic]] layer reflects a genuine
difference in foundational commitments rather than transmission failure, handing
off to [[GMCR]] (§3f). `[STABLE]`
