# Glossary

The following terms carry precise technical meaning throughout this document and
must not be substituted with natural-language synonyms without explicit
redefinition:

- **GMRTI** — General Method for Refinement and Transmission of Ideas
- **GMCR** — General Method for Conflict Resolution (downstream application of
GMRTI)
- **SEMANTIC COVENANT** (*convenio semántico*) — The totality of
meaning-relations held by a rational entity at a given moment; a personal,
circumstantial, and mutable directed graph. Not a fixed dictionary but a living
topology.
- **COMOSÍ** — The functional overlap between two semantic covenants sufficient
to produce apparent understanding within a bounded domain, without guaranteeing
identity of underlying edge-structure.
- **METAAGNOSTICISM** — The epistemological position that: (a) precise
definitions are structurally unavailable due to the circular, self-referential
nature of language-as-symbol-system; and (b) the impossibility of verifying
whether certainty has been achieved is itself a first-order epistemic condition,
not merely a practical limitation. The *meta* in metaagnosticism names precisely
this second clause: not only is certainty unavailable, but the verification of
whether one has achieved it is also unavailable — making the epistemic limit
operate at one level above ordinary fallibilism. `[RECURSIVE][RESTORED]`
- **SEMANTIC ENTROPY** — The log-measure of structural ambiguity in a
transmitted signal, denoted as \(H_s(S)\). Represents the number of
non-isomorphic receiving-covenant edge-configurations compatible with the
signal. In practice, it is operationalized via the **Behavioral Convergence
Proxy (Path-Count Metric)**: the number of independent Polyphonic Encoding paths
that must be successfully traversed and verified by the receiver before they
reach the Operational Action Threshold. `[STABLE]`
- **TRANSMISSION** — The attempted transfer of semantic content from one
covenant to another, or from one temporal state of a covenant to a later state
of the same covenant.
- **REFINEMENT** — The iterative process of improving the internal coherence and
transmissibility of a framework within and across semantic covenants.
- **DIVERGENCE** — Any detectable mismatch between two covenants' handling of
the same conceptual region.
- **FEDERATED GRAPH** — The distributed, externalized semantic structure of an
institution or community, stabilized via Artifact Anchors rather than collective
consciousness.
- **ARTIFACT ANCHOR** — An external, relatively immutable scaffold (written law,
canonical text, standardized protocol) that forces constituent individual
covenants to continuously realign their Structural Subgraphs to a shared
artificial topology.
- **DOMAIN RISK COEFFICIENT (DRC)** — A context-sensitive parameter governing
the required precision of reconstruction; inversely proportional to interpretive
charity.
- **META-DRC DIVERGENCE** — A disagreement between communicating covenants
regarding the DRC classification of a domain, which pauses primary transmission
and defaults to the highest demanded DRC of any participating party until
resolved. `[STABLE]`
- **JUST-IN-TIME ONTOLOGICAL MARKING (JIT-OM)** — The practice of deploying
ontological type distinctions retroactively and locally, only at the precise
site of a detected Edge Divergence, rather than as upfront metalanguage.
- **POLYPHONIC ENCODING** — The deliberate provision of multiple structurally
independent edge-paths to the same semantic destination within a single
transmission act. The primary proactive transmission fidelity strategy.
- **REDUNDANCY-MASKING** — The failure mode in which successful Polyphonic
Encoding conceals a local topological void that would produce transmission
failure under single-path conditions.
- **PATH-STRIPPING PROBING** — The diagnostic protocol for detecting
Redundancy-Masking by temporarily disabling all but one encoding path to check
if the receiver's reconstruction still satisfies the Operational Action
Threshold. `[STABLE]`
- **SYNTACTIC EDGE-FORCING** — A transmission fidelity mechanism in which the
grammatical or compositional structure of an expression compels the receiver to
traverse a specific relational path through semantic space in order to decode
the expression. In the v0.9 prototype, this is implemented by mandating primary
relational operators (e.g. `[->causal]`, `[->inferential]`) in key structural
claims. `[STABLE]`
- **EDGE TAXONOMY** — The partitioned classification of relation-types. Operates
as a two-tiered system: Tier 1 (Causal/Inferential/Other) for initial Covenant
Declaration, and Tier 2 (8-type taxonomy) deployed locally via JIT-OM for
misattribution repair. `[STABLE]`
- **EDGE-TYPE MISATTRIBUTION** — A subtype of Edge Divergence in which both
covenants possess the A-to-B relation but assign it to different edge-types —
one treating it as inferential, the other as causal; one as normative, the other
as affective. Distinct from topological divergence within a shared edge-type,
and requiring a different repair strategy. `[STABLE]`
- **TOPOLOGICAL CONSISTENCY AUDIT** — A diagnostic protocol to detect Bad Actors
by auditing the logical consistency of their declared edge subgraphs and
flagging contradictions or rejection of isomorphic repairs. `[STABLE]`
- **PERIODIC RE-MIRRORING PROTOCOL** — A methodological check to combat Artifact
Anchor decay by periodically evaluating the Federated Graph against the Anchor
under the Mirror posture to produce a desynchronization Delta Graph. `[STABLE]`
- **DEPENDENCY-TRACING SCAN** — A scan of the topological
network that calculates the downstream propagation impact of
altering specific edge-clusters, flagging those above a
configurable node threshold (default 30%, subject to empirical
calibration) as Axiomatically Active. `[STABLE]`
- **BOUNDARY-TRACING ENCAPSULATION** — A formal containment
wrapper drawn around a repaired subgraph that treats it as a
black-box node with declared interfaces, attenuating (not
eliminating) localized meaning-shifts from propagating
holistically across the network. Provides practical isolation
bounded by interface edge count; residual holistic leakage
through interface-edge context change is a known structural
limitation (see 2h.3). `[STABLE]`
- **PRIMITIVE COVENANT** — The GMRTI-specific shared substrate
consisting of the eight primitive relational operators, the
edge-primacy structural commitment, and the Tier 1 coarse
taxonomy. Required for GMRTI-mediated communication
specifically; sits above the framework-independent Minimal
Substrate (2e.5i). Its establishment between agents is itself
a transmission problem. `[STABLE]`
- **MINIMAL SUBSTRATE** — The weakest framework-independent
assumption for structured communication: agents can distinguish
signals from non-signals, detect structural similarity, and
individuate their own conceptual regions. Carries no
GMRTI-specific content. `[STABLE]`
- **MINIMAL INTELLIGIBILITY BOOTSTRAP** — The stratified
decoding substrate: the Minimal Substrate (framework-
independent) guarantees basic signal decoding; the Primitive
Covenant (GMRTI-specific) enables GMRTI-mediated repair.
Calibrating DRC to zero reduces charity to the Minimal
Substrate baseline. `[STABLE]`
- **CORE PATH MINIMIZATION** — The constraint requiring all redundant paths in
polyphonic encoding to be isomorphic translations of a single canonical path,
limiting complexity growth. `[STABLE]`
- **DYNAMIC RE-ANCHORING** — The process of treating Artifact Anchors as a
moving average, updated via continuous correction vectors to manage textual
drift. `[STABLE]`
- **THE MIRROR** — Any Rationally Agnostic receiver (human, LLM, or future-self)
operationalized to reconstruct a transmission using strictly declared edges,
with interpretive charity calibrated to the Domain Risk Coefficient. A tool
within the GMRTI protocol, not the protocol itself.
- **TEMPORAL STRANGER** — The past self treated as a Foreign Mind: the primary
operationalization of Self-to-Future-Self transmission repair.
